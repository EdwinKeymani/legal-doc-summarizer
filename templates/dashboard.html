How these options work

Every upload goes through the same three steps. The options change step 2 and how step 3 is shown.

  1. Read the file. Text is pulled out of the PDF, Word or text file. Scanned PDFs (photos of pages) have no text and will fail.
  2. Summarise it. Extraction Mode picks how, Summary Depth picks how much.
  3. Find and rate risky clauses. Keyword rules find up to one clause each for indemnity, termination, penalties, auto-renewal, governing law and confidentiality. Gemini then rates each one High, Medium or Low and says why. This happens in every mode, and only those clause sentences are sent, not the whole document.
ModeHow the summary is madeSpeedWhole document sent to Google?
Extractive - FastCopies the most central sentences word for word (LexRank)InstantNo
Extractive - PremiumCopies sentences covering the main topics (LSA). Often better on long documentsInstantNo
Abstractive - Premium (AI)Gemini writes a new summary in its own wordsSeconds, longer when busyYes, up to the first ~30,000 characters (about 12 to 15 pages)

Risk Detection Level only changes what the report shows. Every clause is saved, and hidden ones are one click away.

Context Hint tells the AI what kind of document to expect. It is background, not legal expertise for any country, and it has no effect on extractive summaries.

Results are informational and are not legal advice.

AI Configuration

Abstractive summaries and risk assessment are powered by Google Gemini. Document text is sent to Google for these two steps only; extractive summaries run entirely on this server. The API key is read from the server environment and is never shown here. If the AI service is busy or unavailable, clauses are marked "Review" and can be re-assessed from the report page.
